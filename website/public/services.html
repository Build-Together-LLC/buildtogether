FAQ

Common questions

How long does an ERPNext implementation take?

A focused rollout for a small or mid-size business typically takes 6–12 weeks. Larger multi-company or manufacturing projects take longer. You get a fixed timeline after discovery.

Will customisations break when ERPNext upgrades?

Not if they're built properly. We ship everything as a separate Frappe app using hooks, custom fields and fixtures, without editing core code, so upgrades stay predictable.

Do you work with US-based companies?

Yes. A large share of our clients are in the US. We keep several hours of daily overlap with US business hours and handle US-specific needs such as sales tax and multi-currency.

Can you take over an existing ERPNext system?

Yes. We start with an audit of your current setup, customisations and data quality, then propose a stabilisation and improvement plan.

Do we need to use AI to work with you?

No. Many clients start with a straightforward implementation or customisation and add AI automations later, once the foundation is solid.
