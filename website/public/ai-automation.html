Built responsibly

Enterprise-grade AI you can trust with your books

AI in an ERP has to be accurate and accountable. Every solution we build follows these principles.

  • Your data stays yoursEnterprise model APIs with no training on your data, or private and self-hosted models where needed.
  • Human-in-the-loopAI drafts and people approve. You set the confidence thresholds and approval rules.
  • Respects ERPNext permissionsAgents act with the same roles and restrictions as the user they work for.
  • Fully auditableEvery AI action is logged with its inputs, outputs and the approving user.
