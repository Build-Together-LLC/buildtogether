Our story

Engineers first, consultants second

We started BuildTogether as software developers who kept seeing the same problem. Businesses bought powerful ERP systems, but they were set up generically, customised poorly, and never connected to the AI tools that could save their teams hours every day.

So we chose to specialise. We went deep on Frappe and ERPNext, applied our background in data science and AI, and built a company where every project is engineered properly, documented clearly and designed to keep improving.
